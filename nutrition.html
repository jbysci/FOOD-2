Vitamins & Minerals

Eat a variety of colourful fruits and vegetables to ensure you're getting a wide range of vitamins and minerals. Dark leafy greens, citrus fruits, and berries are especially nutrient-dense.

💧

Hydration

Aim for around 2 litres of water per day. Herbal teas, fruits with high water content, and soups also contribute to your daily hydration needs.

🫒

Healthy Fats

Include sources of healthy fats like avocados, nuts, seeds, and olive oil. These support brain function and help absorb fat-soluble vitamins.

🍚

Balanced Meals

Try to build each meal around a source of protein, complex carbohydrates, and vegetables. This helps maintain steady energy levels throughout the day.
